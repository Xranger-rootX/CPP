Best & Efficient Software Stack for MacBook M4 Air
Screen Recording • Video Editing • Thumbnails • Voiceover
(for project walkthroughs & uni reports)

All tools below are Apple Silicon native — fast and battery-friendly.

=====================================================
🎥 SCREEN RECORDING
=====================================================
Best pick: OBS Studio (free)
- Apple Silicon native, unlimited recording
- Scenes/camera overlays for project walkthroughs
- macOS built-in recorder (Cmd + Shift + 5) is a zero-setup
  alternative, great for quick uni report captures, but no
  camera overlay or scene switching.

Settings tip: Record at 1080p or 4K, 30fps is fine for
tutorials (60fps only if showing fast motion/games).

=====================================================
✂️ VIDEO EDITING
=====================================================
Best pick: DaVinci Resolve (free)
- Apple Silicon native, uses the M4's media engine
- Hollywood-grade; the free version has everything you need
Alternative: iMovie (free, pre-installed)
- Fine for simple uni reports with minimal editing,
  but you'll outgrow it fast.

M4 Air note: Resolve runs well, but for very long 4K edits
consider exporting at 1080p to keep the fanless Air cool.

=====================================================
🖼️ THUMBNAILS
=====================================================
Best pick: Canva (free tier)
- Templates, drag-and-drop, exports YouTube-ready 1280x720
Alternative: Photopea (free, browser-based)
- Essentially free Photoshop in a browser; better if you
  want pixel-level control.

=====================================================
🎙️ VOICEOVER
=====================================================
Best pick: MacBook's built-in mic + free tools to start.
The M4 Air's mics are surprisingly decent. Two free apps
make a big difference:
- Audacity (free) — record + noise reduction + EQ
- Adobe Podcast Enhance (free, browser) — paste your audio,
  AI cleans background noise instantly.

Cheap upgrade that beats any software: a $20-50 USB lav
mic or dynamic mic (e.g., Fifine or Samson Q2U).

If you want an AI voice instead: ElevenLabs (best quality,
~$5/mo) — but disclose it in the description.

=====================================================
⚙️ QUICK SETUP TIPS FOR M4 AIR
=====================================================
1. External SSD or >=512GB storage — video files eat space
   fast (~1GB per 10 min at 1080p).
2. OBS: Settings -> Output -> use Apple silicon hardware
   encoder (VideoToolbox) — smooth recording without
   taxing the CPU.
3. DaVinci Resolve: enable "Optimized Media" or record in
   ProRes for smoother editing timelines.
4. Record in a quiet room; a blanket on the wall behind you
   = instantly better audio.

=====================================================
🚀 FIRST-VIDEO WORKFLOW (record -> upload)
=====================================================
1. Pick a searchable topic (YouTube autocomplete /
   Google Trends).
2. Write a bullet outline — don't script word-for-word.
3. Record screen + voiceover in OBS (one take is fine).
4. Edit in Resolve: cut dead air, zoom on key moments,
   add captions.
5. Make thumbnail in Canva: 3-5 words, high contrast.
6. Upload with keyword-rich title/description, pin a
   comment. Done beats perfect.
